Weighted-bias edge weight — how much a win is worth

weight w = 1 + x·|T_A − T_B| / (10·G), team A always wins every game (T_A = 10·G); the x-axis is the opponent's goal total T_B.

Reading it: a shutout (T_B = 0) is worth the full 1 + x; the weight falls linearly to just above the base credit 1 as the opponent keeps up. The slope is what x controls — at x = 1 domination barely matters, at x = 8 a sweep counts nearly nine times a nail-biter. N.B. the right panel is partly theoretical: in a real best-of-three a 3-game winner has conceded a game, so T_A ≤ 29 and T_B ≥ 10 — real matches live on the right half of that panel.
